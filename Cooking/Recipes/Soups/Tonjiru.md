> [!NOTE] Note
> Tonjiru is actually a **vegetable-heavy soup**, and the pork is traditionally used more like a seasoning or a small accent rather than the main star. Even when eaten as a main dish, the volume is usually made up of massive amounts of root vegetables (like daikon, burdock root, and taro) rather than a mountain of meat. Expected: 200g of pork for 1L of stock
