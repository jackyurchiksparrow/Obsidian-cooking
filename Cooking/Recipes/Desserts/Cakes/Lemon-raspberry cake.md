| **-- 1. Zest 5 lemons on the fine side of the grater, taking only the yellow and none of the white pith. Rub the zest into the sugar with your fingers and leave it 20 min — the crystals abrade the oil cells and pull more aroma out than heat alone will. --**<br><br>**-- 2. Juice enough lemons for 333 g total (228 g cream + 105 g syrup). Budget 8-9 lemons; yield runs 45-57 g each. --**<br><br>**-- 3. Bring the remaining milk with the zested sugar to steaming in a thick-bottomed pan. --**<br><br>**-- 5. Temper the yolk mixture in, then cook on the lowest heat until it thickens substantially. Hot cream is fluid and the blender bites; cold it is a stiff paste and you will only smear it. And this must happen before the butter — a butter cream is an emulsion held by whipping, and high-shear blending will warm it and break it._<br><br>**-- 7.
