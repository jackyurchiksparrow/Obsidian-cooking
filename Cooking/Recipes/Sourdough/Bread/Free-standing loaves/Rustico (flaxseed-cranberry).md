> [!Attention] Remember!
> - Knead by hand, spelt is fragile
> - Spelt's gluten is extensible and fragile — it hydrates fast, develops fast, and then degrades fast. It has very little tolerance for extended fermentation; it goes from silky to soupy over about twenty minutes.
