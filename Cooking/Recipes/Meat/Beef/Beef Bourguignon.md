> [!Attention] Suitable cuts of meat
> | Ukrainian                   | English                              | Notes                                                                                  |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Лопатка**                 | Chuck / shoulder (French: _paleron_) | The classic. Good marbling and collagen, easy to cut into even cubes                   |
| **Підлопаткова частина**    | Chuck roll / chuck eye               | Part of the shoulder, a bit more marbled. Excellent                                    |
| **Шия**                     | Neck                                 | Very flavourful and gelatinous, often cheap                                            |
| **Голяшка**                 | Shank (French: _jarret_)             | The most gelatin of all, gives the silkiest sauce. Lean, so it pairs well with лопатка |
| **Щоки**                    | Cheeks (French: _joue_)              | Probably the best texture of all, but harder to find                                   |
| **Грудинка**                | Brisket                              | Works, but it’s fattier and more layered                                               |
| **Короткі ребра / реберця** | Short ribs                           | Rich and fatty. Good, but the bones take up space in the pot                           |
| **Хвіст**                   | Oxtail                               | Fantastic for the sauce, but there’s little meat on it. Better as an addition          |
