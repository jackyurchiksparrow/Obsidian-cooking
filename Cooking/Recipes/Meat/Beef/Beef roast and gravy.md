## Directions:

- the softest cut of beef you can find, whole, trimmed, pat dried, shaped into an even slice (ideally with cooking strings)
- a generous amount of pre-toasted whole spices, grounded and applied to dry beef
- olive oil
- salt
- mustard, crashed coriander seeds
- vegetables to taste: celery stalks (3-4), 1 onion, whole head of garlic, a carrot, a few bay leaves
- fresh herbs you like
- cider/wine + jam/wine
- tapioca starch/flour if needed.

1. Shape with a baking string and make sure it's been out of the fridge for at least an hour. 
2. Apply spices to the beef. Then apply olive oil with salt and sear the sides.
3. Slice vegies, including big heads of garlic. Place them into the same pan and cover with the remaining oil from the sear. 
4. Put the seared beef on top of the vegies and apply an additional portion of olive oil.
5. Bake until about 52 C (almost medium rare), about 40 minutes. Then let it rest for as long as it cooked.
6. Heat the meat and vegetable juices from the oven, add fresh herbs. (You may blend some vegies with the stock and add there). Add cider/wine, reduce strongly. Add stock, reduce. 
7. Add tapioca starch or flour if needed. Mash, put through a sieve.
8. Long-thinly slice the beef, place into boiling sauce for a minute or until looks cooked, while constantly basting. Serve hot.
